MUSIC
My Story

Music has been with me since the very start. Growing up, I was raised on the sounds of Tiesto, Avicii, Skrillex, and Daft Punk — electronic music ran through everything. My idol is Skrillex. The first track I ever heard from him was Ruffneck (Full Flex) from the More Monsters and Sprites EP. That moment stuck.

As a kid I was always into something: learning English, drawing, picking up instruments. But music production came properly at age 12, on my dad's old office desktop. I started on FL Studio before switching to Ableton. At 16, I began releasing music as Keari — though nothing officially dropped until now.

This year changes that. I'm publishing a wave of unreleased tracks built across 2024–25, plus fresh material. The catalog is finally seeing the light.

Outside of music, I've also been picking up photography. Still early days, but I love capturing moments and cool shots — most of what I shoot ends up on my Instagram and TikTok. It's another creative outlet that keeps growing alongside everything else.
